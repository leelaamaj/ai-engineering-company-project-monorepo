# Company Choice: TrackFlow

## Why I Chose TrackFlow

I chose TrackFlow because I enjoy connecting people, systems, and information to solve real-world problems. The company has practical challenges involving inventory, orders, deliveries, and customer service, so the results of each project will be easy to understand and measure. I am also interested in helping its Los Angeles and Zaragoza teams share accurate information instead of relying on separate systems and manual work. TrackFlow will allow me to practice building useful AI automations while learning how an international logistics company operates.

## Departments That Interest Me

### Technology

The Technology department interests me because it connects and monitors the systems used throughout the company. Improving its old integrations, documentation, monitoring, and alerts could help every other department work more reliably.

### Warehouse Operations

Warehouse Operations interests me because accurate inventory and order information are the foundation of the delivery process. Connecting both warehouses through a unified inventory system could reduce transcription mistakes, missing stock, and delayed orders.

## Automation Challenge

The automation challenge I am most excited to build is a unified inventory system that displays real-time stock for both warehouses and automatically warns employees when an item is running low.

## My AI Agent Idea

My AI agent would monitor incoming orders and inventory levels across both warehouses. It would need information from order emails, warehouse inventory systems, product records, and low-stock limits. The agent would organize incoming order data, detect missing or conflicting information, and compare requested items with available stock. It would produce clear alerts for employees and trigger a low-stock notification when inventory falls below the required level, helping the team respond quickly and keep deliveries moving.
